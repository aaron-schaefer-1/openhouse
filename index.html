What This Is About

We're hosting an open house every couple of weeks all summer long — a standing invitation to come over, slow down, and spend time with people.

There's no agenda. Come and go as you please. Anyone is welcome — feel free to bring a friend.

Kids are extremely welcome. Our backyard pool, hot tub, and movie room are all open and ready.

On the Menu

Lunch can be any time — we'll have burgers and hot dogs available all afternoon.

You're welcome to bring a side, chips, snack, dessert, or drinks if you'd like to contribute.

Feel free to use our kitchen to prepare your own food if you need to.

Have something you'd like grilled? Bring it over — I'm happy to throw anything you want on the grill.

Bringing Friends

Absolutely — the more the merrier!

The easiest way to share is with the "Share Invite" link or QR code at the top of the page. Just tap it and send it along.

The only thing we ask is that they RSVP so we have a rough head count for food.

Kids & Our Backyard Pool
  • Kids of all ages are extremely welcome
  • The movie room will be open all afternoon playing kid-friendly movies
  • Our backyard pool is open — parents please supervise your own children at all times
  • Bring swimsuits, towels, and sunscreen
